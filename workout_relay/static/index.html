Before connecting

This uses an unofficial Garmin integration. Your credentials pass through Workout Relay once and are not stored. Encrypted session tokens provide ongoing account access.

Because the operator controls the hosted server, use this service only if you trust its operator. Encryption protects stored data, but cannot protect against malicious server code.

How long may we keep your Garmin session?

Enter the verification code Garmin sent you. This attempt expires after five minutes.

Workout Relay stores only what it needs to work: your account email, a password hash, and the plans you send. Cookies here are strictly necessary for signing in, so there is nothing to opt into. You choose where your Garmin session is kept when you connect it.

Privacy

What is stored, and why

Kept

  • Your account email and a password hash, so you can sign in.
  • Plans you send and their results, deleted automatically after the retention period.
  • Encrypted Garmin session tokens — only if you chose "Keep Garmin connected".
  • Which assistants you connected, and when they last acted.

Never kept

  • Your Garmin email and password, or MFA codes.
  • Garmin tokens in readable form, or in the database at all under "This visit only".
  • Analytics, advertising or third-party tracking of any kind.

Your controls

  • Disconnect Garmin at any time, or reconnect to change where the session is kept.
  • Disconnect an assistant under Assistants; it loses access immediately.
  • Delete your account under Account, which removes everything above.

Cookies: one signed-in session cookie and one CSRF cookie, both strictly necessary, plus your language choice stored in this browser. No consent banner is needed for those, and nothing else is set.
