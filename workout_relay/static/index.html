Set up

Connect Garmin to send your first plan

You sign in to Garmin once, here. There is nothing to copy from Garmin Connect: Workout Relay uses your Garmin account sign-in, and never stores the password.

  1. Check how you sign in to Garmin

    You need your Garmin email and password. If you sign in to Garmin with Apple or Google instead, there is no password for us to use: open Garmin account settings and set one first. This is the most common reason connecting fails.

  2. Enter the verification code, if asked

    If your Garmin account uses two-factor authentication, Garmin sends a code by email or text. Type it on the next screen. The attempt expires after five minutes; start again if it does.

  3. Choose how long we keep the session

    Keep Garmin connected so plans can be sent later, including by an assistant while you are away. Or choose this visit only, and nothing Garmin-related is stored at all.

  4. Find the workout in Garmin Connect

    After sending a plan, open Garmin Connect and look at the calendar on the workout's date. It syncs to your watch the same way a workout you created there would.

Need help connecting?

You need your Garmin email and password. If you sign in to Garmin with Apple or Google instead, there is no password for us to use: open Garmin account settings and set one first. This is the most common reason connecting fails.

If your Garmin account uses two-factor authentication, Garmin sends a code by email or text. Type it on the next screen. The attempt expires after five minutes; start again if it does.

Before connecting

This uses an unofficial Garmin integration. Your credentials pass through Workout Relay once and are not stored. Encrypted session tokens provide ongoing account access.

Because the operator controls the hosted server, use this service only if you trust its operator. Encryption protects stored data, but cannot protect against malicious server code.

How long may we keep your Garmin session?

Enter the verification code Garmin sent you. This attempt expires after five minutes.

Workout Relay stores only what it needs to work: your account email, a password hash, and the plans you send. Cookies here are strictly necessary for signing in, so there is nothing to opt into. You choose where your Garmin session is kept when you connect it.

Privacy

What is stored, and why

Kept

  • Your account email and a password hash, so you can sign in.
  • Plans you send and their results, deleted automatically after the retention period. Clear history under Plans removes them sooner.
  • Encrypted Garmin session tokens — only if you chose "Keep Garmin connected".
  • Which assistants you connected, and when they last acted.
  • If you granted activity access: the IDs of activities shown to an assistant, with your Garmin account name, kept briefly so it can only fetch what it was shown. The measurements themselves are passed straight through and never stored.
  • A hashed record of each signed-in browser session, and your language choice.

Never kept

  • Your Garmin email and password, or MFA codes.
  • Garmin tokens in readable form, or in the database at all under "This visit only".
  • Analytics, advertising or third-party tracking of any kind.

Your controls

  • Disconnect Garmin at any time, or reconnect to change where the session is kept.
  • Disconnect an assistant under Assistants; it loses access immediately.
  • Delete your account under Account, which removes everything above.

Cookies: one signed-in session cookie and one CSRF cookie, both strictly necessary, plus your language choice stored in this browser. No consent banner is needed for those, and nothing else is set.
